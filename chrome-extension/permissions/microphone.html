Microphone access

Voice input and voice commands need your microphone. Chrome can only ask for it from a normal tab, not from the side panel — so grant it here once and the side panel keeps working.

Audio is sent to your browser's speech recognition service for transcription. AI Toolbox never records or stores it.

Check it works

Press Start, say a few words, and they should appear below. If nothing appears here either, speech recognition is unavailable in this browser rather than blocked in the side panel.
